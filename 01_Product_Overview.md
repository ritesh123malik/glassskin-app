# GLASSSKIN Mobile Application - Product Overview

## Problem Statement
GLASSSKIN, a beauty and personal care e-commerce brand offering natural, toxin-free skincare and personal care products, currently operates without a dedicated mobile application. Customers must access the brand through web browsers, resulting in a suboptimal shopping experience that doesn't reflect the brand's premium, clean aesthetic. This limits customer engagement, retention, and sales conversion opportunities in an increasingly mobile-first commerce landscape.

## Solution Summary
Develop a native/cross-platform React Native mobile application for GLASSSKIN that provides a seamless, visually appealing shopping experience. The app will feature product browsing, search, filtering, cart management, wishlist functionality, secure checkout with multiple payment options, order tracking, and push notifications for order updates and promotional content.

## Target Audience
- Primary: Women aged 25-45 who value clean beauty, natural skincare, and premium personal care products
- Secondary: Beauty enthusiasts seeking toxin-free alternatives to conventional cosmetics
- Tertiary: Existing GLASSSKIN web customers looking for a more convenient shopping experience

## Core Value Proposition
A premium mobile shopping experience that embodies GLASSSKIN's "clean beauty" ethos through a visually stunning glassmorphism design, making it easy for customers to discover, purchase, and track their favorite natural skincare and personal care products.

## Measurable Success Goals
| Goal | Metric | Target | Timeline |
|------|--------|--------|----------|
| App Adoption | Number of app downloads | 5,000 downloads | 6 months post-launch |
| Conversion Rate | Purchases per active user | 15% conversion rate | 3 months post-launch |
