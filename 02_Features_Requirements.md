# Features & Requirements

# Functional Requirements

## Priority 1: Core E-commerce Features

### Product Catalog & Discovery
- Display product listings with images, names, prices, and ratings
- Implement product detail pages with descriptions, ingredients, usage instructions, and reviews
- Enable product search with real-time filtering as user types
- Provide category-based browsing (Skincare, Hair Care, Body Care, etc.)
- Implement advanced filtering by price range, product type, skin type, and certifications

### Shopping Cart & Wishlist
- Add/remove products to/from cart with quantity adjustment
- Persist cart items across sessions using Supabase
- Save products to wishlist for later purchase
- Display wishlist items with quick-add-to-cart functionality

### User Authentication
- Email/password registration and login
- Social login via Google and Apple
- Password reset functionality
- Guest checkout option without mandatory account creation

### Secure Checkout
- Multi-step checkout flow: shipping address, payment method, order review
- Support for credit/debit cards via Stripe integration
- Support for PayPal payments
- Apply promo codes and discounts
- Order confirmation with summary and next steps

### Order Management
- View order history with status indicators
- Track order progress (Processing, Shipped, Delivered)
- Re-order previous purchases with one tap
- Cancel orders before shipment confirmation

## Priority 2: Engagement Features

### Push Notifications
- Order status updates (confirmation, processing, shipped, delivered)
- Promotional notifications for new products and sales
- Personalized recommendations based on purchase history
- Abandoned cart reminders

### User Profile
- Personal information management (name, email, shipping addresses)
- Payment method storage and management
- Order history and tracking
- Wishlist management

## Priority 3: Enhancement Features

### Product Reviews & Ratings
- Submit product reviews with star ratings and text
- View aggregate ratings and individual reviews
- Photo upload capability for reviews
- Helpful/not helpful voting system

### Search & Recommendations
- Intelligent search with autocomplete suggestions
- "Frequently bought together" recommendations
- "Customers also viewed" suggestions
- Personalized product recommendations

### Analytics & Insights
- View personal purchase statistics
- Track spending over time
- Favorite categories and brands
