## Privacy Policy (Draft)

**Last Updated:** [Date]

**1. Information We Collect**
- **Personal Information:** When you create an account, we collect your name, email address, and optionally a phone number.
- **Order Information:** We collect shipping addresses to fulfill orders.
- **Payment Information:** We do not collect or store your full credit card number or CVV. All payment processing is securely handled directly by our third-party payment processors (e.g., Stripe, PayPal).
- **Device & Usage Data:** We collect analytics data (via PostHog/Sentry) to improve app performance, including device identifiers, OS versions, and crash reports. We explicitly scrub PII (Personally Identifiable Information) from crash reports before transmission.

**2. How We Use Your Information**
- To process and fulfill your orders.
- To communicate with you regarding order status, promotions (if opted in), and support.
- To improve our application's performance and fix bugs.

**3. Data Sharing and Disclosure**
We do not sell your personal data. We share your data only with essential service providers:
- Payment processors (Stripe, PayPal) to process transactions.
- Infrastructure providers (Supabase) to securely host data.
- Delivery partners to ship your physical orders.

**4. Your Rights (GDPR / CCPA Compliance)**
You have the right to:
- **Access and Portability:** Request a copy of your personal data. You can export your data anytime within the app via the Settings > Export Data button.
- **Deletion ("Right to be Forgotten"):** Request deletion of your account. You can permanently delete your account directly within the app via Settings > Delete Account. Please note that we must retain basic order information for tax and accounting purposes.
- **Opt-out:** Opt out of promotional communications at any time.
